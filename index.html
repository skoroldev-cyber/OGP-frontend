The Immersive Reading Room

The Immersive Reading Room is a continuity environment for human recognition. It presents the Opening Arc with complete typographic authority: the manuscript text is rendered exactly as authored, without transformation. Reading requires no account, and reading progress is preserved without one.

The experience is one continuous application. A visitor arrives to darkness, watches the Living Weave manifest, passes through it as a threshold, encounters Earth as it actually is — borderless, luminous, and alive — and is then invited to read.

A skip path, a reduced-motion path, and a silent path are available throughout. Audio is strictly opt-in. The experience targets WCAG 2.2 Level AA.
